## 2. Dynamic Value & Indicator Styling Rules

The newly added **Avg TTR Days** card incorporates safe rendering boundaries to deliver high-quality aesthetics and prevent misleading zero-states:

*   **Zero/Negative Boundary**: When `avgTtrDays` is less than or equal to `0.0` (or `null`), the card displays `N/A` instead of a misleading `0.0 days`.
*   **Active KPI State**: Shows the exact decimal with the suffix ` days` (e.g. `4.2 days`).
*   **PillChip Dynamic Color Indicators**:
    *   `<= 7.0` days: Rendered as a green `PillChip` labeled **"Fast"** (On Track).
    *   `<= 14.0` days: Rendered as a yellow `PillChip` labeled **"On Pace"** (Acceptable).
    *   `> 14.0` days: Rendered as a red `PillChip` labeled **"Slow"** (Critical).
